Macro estimate

Source of the estimates: macro estimate, not yet the team's Set in P2 by the Architect and the SM, from the size of the stories. When the team has estimated the stories in the project tool (Jira or other, depending on the framework configuration), its estimates will replace these and the schedule will adjust. Until then, every value is an assumption.
Delivery by mode
Human: a developer without assistance, in working days (d). AI-assisted: the same with a coding assistant. Autonomous AI: the agents' working hours (h)
Validation, acceptance
the human effort that remains in every mode, in days: business validation of each story, gates, acceptance
Work outside stories
design, foundation, QA campaign and acceptance: their values come from the schedule configuration, so they are written only once. Design and the automated QA campaign are durations, not team effort
Scale
story values are computed from their size; the settings button, top right of the section, changes the scale (in your browser only) and the schedule follows
Front
the share of the story's work on the front end, in %, the rest on the back end; it splits the effort between front-end, back-end and full stack developers in the schedule
Size
XS to XL, the relative complexity, which does not depend on the mode. Story values go from cold (low) to hot (high), column by column; work outside stories is not colored.

Team composition

What it is
Who does the work, with their presence in full-time equivalents: 1.5 = one person full time and one half time.
By mode
The dev team serves the Human and AI-assisted human modes. The PO and the acceptance helpers serve every mode. The number of agents is set in the schedule settings.
One more, one less
The effect of one full-time more or less on the date of the last release, in each mode. In Autonomous AI, only the PO and the acceptance helpers count: developers are not in it.
Your trials
Your changes stay in your browser.

Delivery schedule

The calculation
Scheduling under capacity constraints. In priority order, each story starts as soon as its dependencies are done, on the free people who have the skill.
The dates
Each estimate (stories, work, durations of design and QA campaign) is drawn at random within its range (at best, probable, at worst), over several hundred simulations; deliveries from other teams stay at their date. Each release has a probable date (50% chance of being ready before) and a prudent date (85%).
The settings
One tab per mode. The team comes from the Team composition section; the Settings block sets the dates, the other teams and the calculation rules.
Your trials
They stay in your browser. The reference configuration is in the page.
Reading
Click a group row of the schedule to see the detail.
An estimation aid, not a commitment This schedule simulates a plan from estimates, capacities and dependencies that are guessed. It serves to compare team scenarios and to spot bottlenecks; its dates are not reliable enough to commit to the business.
Assumptions
  • The team, its capacities and the dependencies between stories are guessed.
  • delivery dates of the external teams to confirm with them
  • Design (P1 to P3) is a duration: the BA, the UX and the Architect run it outside the dev team, which does not shorten it. In Autonomous AI, the agents do.

Calibration

After each sprint, the SM adds the estimated time and the actual time of the finished stories, in agent hours, to the schedule configuration (rules.calibration.sprints). The actual over estimated ratio corrects every story estimate; it can be forced in the schedule settings for a trial.
