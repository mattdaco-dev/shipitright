Open points

Site map

Purpose
See how the detailed design is organized and go straight to a page.
Content
The tree of pages, from the macro design (entry point) to the summary, then to the page families. One block per page, its title as a link; a family (endpoints, schemas, entities, processes) fits in one block of stacked cards whose title opens the list of its pages.
Why this formalism
The tree is the standard form of a site map. The sections of a page are not in it: they are in its table of contents.
Reading
  • gen generated page: it is not edited, one changes openapi.yaml and regenerates.
  • frozen contract the operation loses nothing and changes nothing: the front starts on a mock.
  • provisional contract the operation waits for a business answer.
  • The release is indicative.
  • API conventions of the project: the ADR that sets them, as a link.
Source
The actual pages of the folder, read by the generator: the map never lies.
Coding an endpoint
  1. Its page: request, responses, behaviour, sequence.
  2. Its processes: signature, algorithm, edge cases.
  3. Its entities: DDL.
  4. The code organization: where to put it.
  5. The compliance contract: the invariants.
  6. The required tests.
Coding a screen
  1. The by-screen view.
  2. The pages of its endpoints.
  3. The error handling.

Endpoints

Purpose
Find an operation of the API contract by its path, its permissions or its status.
Content
One row per operation: identifier, verb and path, summary, allowed roles, frozen or provisional status. The release, indicative, is on the page of the endpoint.
Why this formalism
The list of the OpenAPI operations as a sortable table, reduced to what helps find an operation.
Source
openapi.yaml.

CRUD matrix

Purpose
Know who creates, reads, updates or deletes which entity; it is also the index of the entities and processes.
Content
In rows the endpoints then the processes, in columns the entities; the last column gives the processes called.
Why this formalism
The CRUD matrix, standard artefact of the data × processes cross-reference.
Reading
C create, R read, U update, D delete. For an endpoint, a cell also counts the access it obtains through a process, as it declares it (a process that creates or modifies gives only C to the one that creates); hovering says through which process.
Source
x-entities of openapi.yaml (direct access, or obtained via a process) and x-processes; meta tags of the processes for their rows.
