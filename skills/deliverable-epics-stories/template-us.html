Definition of Ready

  • A single actor in the need, and the "so that" can be checked at the story's acceptance
  • Each rule is declarative; shared rules cite their BR-xx
  • Scenarios are numbered, named, complete, without test data
  • Nominal, blocked case, each actor, each flow and the error state are covered
  • Labels are word for word, consistent with the functional design
  • Each screen touched is cited, reachable in the prototype, with its screenshot
  • No blocking point to settle; each open question carries its Q-xxx badge
  • Size XS to L (an XL story is split before entering a sprint, and before G1 for a release presented at G1), front share filled, dependencies listed
  • Technical notes and endpoints filled by the Architect (required before sprint planning)
